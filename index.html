Diagnose — Identify Gaps, Friction & Root Causes

Through comprehensive on-site quality audits, mystery guest observations, workflow shadow reviews, and team interviews, we pinpoint root causes rather than superficial symptoms.

✦
Process & Productivity Diagnostics: Identifying bottlenecks, redundant steps, and labor friction.

We architect practical systems, modernized SOPs, quality checklists, and touchpoint standards that your team will actually execute consistently every day.

✦

Executive Audit Summary: Boutique Heritage Hotel

Conducted by IQA Hospitality Advisory

89.4% Overall Index
1. Key Strengths Identified
  • Front Office Warmth: High emotional quotient during check-in; staff consistently used guest names and offered tailored local recommendations.
  • F&B Breakfast Quality: Exceptional live station presentation, high food hygiene score (98%), and prompt beverage replenishment.
  • Ambiance & Scent Architecture: Consistent sensory branding throughout public lobbies and guest corridors.
2. Priority Gaps & Vulnerabilities
  • Housekeeping Turn-Down Inconsistency: 3 out of 5 audited rooms lacked secondary pillow placement; inconsistent replenishment of premium bath amenities.
  • Service Recovery Pacing: Front desk escalations for billing queries averaged 14 minutes due to supervisor absence on the floor.
  • Kitchen-to-Floor Communication: Dinner peak ticket pacing exceeded 24 minutes for main courses due to uncoordinated pass management.
3. 30/60/90-Day Improvement Roadmap Summary
Days 1–30
Immediate Fixes

Roll out daily housekeeping turn-down checklist; establish 5-minute frontline service recovery empowerment threshold.

Days 31–60
Capability & Training

Conduct 2-day Supervisory Excellence workshop for shift leads; restructure restaurant pass expediter role.

Days 61–90
Verification & Audit

Conduct follow-up mystery audit; calibrate internal supervisory scorecards for perpetual consistency.
